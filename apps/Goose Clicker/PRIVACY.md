# Goose Clicker Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 16, 2026**

Goose Clicker does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Period.

## What we collect

Nothing.

- No personal information
- No usage analytics or tracking
- No advertising identifiers
- No location data
- No third-party SDKs that collect data

## Where your game data lives

Your game progress (like your shareholder value) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Network access

Goose Clicker does not connect to the internet. There are no accounts, no servers, and no data transmission of any kind.

## Children's privacy

Since we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how the app handles data, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised.
