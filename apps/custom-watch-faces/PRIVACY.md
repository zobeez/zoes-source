# Custom Watch Faces — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 17, 2026_

Custom Watch Faces does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

## What happens on your device

Everything the app does — displaying watch faces, keeping the screen awake, and remembering which face you picked — happens entirely on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Nothing ever leaves your devices.

## Network requests

The app makes no network requests at all.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on this repository.
