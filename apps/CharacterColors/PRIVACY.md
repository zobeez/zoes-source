# Hex Code Getter — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 16, 2026_

Hex Code Getter does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

## What happens on your device

Everything the app does with your images — background removal, color extraction, and building palettes — happens entirely on your device. Your photos and the characters you save never leave it.

When you add a photo, the app uses the system photo picker, so it only ever sees the single image you choose — it has no access to the rest of your photo library.

## Network requests

Two optional features fetch content from the internet:

- **Search by name** sends your search term to DuckDuckGo to find a matching image.
- **Wikipedia import** sends the article title or link you provide to Wikipedia to fetch the article's image.

In both cases, only the text you typed is sent — nothing else. These requests are subject to the privacy policies of [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/privacy) and the [Wikimedia Foundation](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy). If you don't use these features, the app makes no network requests at all.
